Geometric Insight

Leaning top: gravity produces a sideways twist instead of straight downward motion.

  • COM offset: \(d = h \sin\theta\)
  • Torque: \(\tau = Mg h \sin\theta\) (perpendicular to spin axis)
  • Angular momentum: \(L = I\omega\) along tilted axis

Torque changes the direction of \(L\), not its magnitude → axis rotates (precession).

This motion is called precession. Relation between \(\tau\), \(L\), and precession rate follows next.
