Geometric Insight

When the top leans, gravity produces a twisting effect that acts sideways rather than straight down.

  • The center of mass is horizontally offset by \(d = h \sin\theta\) from the pivot.
  • Gravity \(Mg\) creates a torque \(\tau = Mg h \sin\theta\), acting perpendicular to the spin axis.
  • The spinning top has angular momentum \(L = I\omega\) directed along its tilted axis.

Torque changes the direction of angular momentum, not its magnitude. As a result, the angular momentum vector slowly rotates, causing the top’s axis to precess around the vertical instead of falling over.
